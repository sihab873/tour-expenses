    • Add expense when someone pays for something: a bus, food, the resort. Choose who it was for and the app splits it equally.
    • Money given when cash moves between people and nothing is bought: an advance to whoever is managing money, or paying someone back.

    • Only me expenses are just for your own record and never change anyone's balance.
